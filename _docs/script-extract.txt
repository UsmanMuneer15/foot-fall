1. PURPOSE OF THIS DOCUMENT
This document is the final content and creative-direction brief for the FOOTFALL GLOBAL LLC website. It should be
used by the web developer, UI/UX designer, content team and motion designer as the primary reference for completing
the site.
The website must sell the FOOTFALL idea before it sells equipment. FOOTFALL is an experiential engagement and
audience-attraction company. Its products, technology and event infrastructure are the tools used to create the
experience.
2. BRAND FOUNDATION
Company: FOOTFALL GLOBAL LLC
Proprietor: Amal El Houm
Locked master slogan: TURNING SPACES INTO DESTINATIONS.
Core business: Experiential engagement, event activations, audience attraction, event technology and measurable
visitor interaction.
The master slogan must be used exactly as written. Do not replace it with another primary slogan unless specifically
approved.
3. THE FOOTFALL POSITIONING
FOOTFALL GLOBAL exists to create a reason for people to approach a particular place, brand, stand, venue or event
zone. The company develops experiences that attract attention, encourage participation and create a memorable
connection between the audience and the brand.
The positioning is not: an event-equipment rental company that also creates activations.
The positioning is: an experiential engagement company that controls the concepts, technology, equipment and
infrastructure required to deliver those experiences.
The central philosophy is: Experience instead of direct selling.
4. BRAND EXPERIENCE MODEL
Use the following engagement journey throughout the site:
ATTRACT -> ENGAGE -> EXPERIENCE -> CAPTURE -> CONNECT
ATTRACT: Create something visually difficult to ignore.
ENGAGE: Give people a reason to approach and participate.
EXPERIENCE: Make the interaction memorable and relevant to the brand.
CAPTURE: Where appropriate, use registration, QR, touchscreen or event technology to measure engagement.
CONNECT: Extend the relationship beyond the physical activation.
5. VISUAL IDENTITY - LOCKED DIRECTION
The website must follow the FOOTFALL visual identity already established by the logo and approved concept.
Primary background: Deep luxury green.
Primary accent: Metallic / warm gold.
Supporting tones: Off-white, muted cream, charcoal and controlled black.
Logo treatment: Gold logo on deep green wherever practical.

6. HOMEPAGE - HERO SECTION
The first screen must communicate the company in seconds.
Primary headline:
TURNING SPACES INTO DESTINATIONS.
Supporting copy:
We create experiences that attract people, inspire participation and turn ordinary spaces into places people want to be.
Primary CTA: EXPLORE EXPERIENCES
Secondary CTA: CREATE AN ACTIVATION
The hero should use a strong full-width image or short cinematic loop showing a FOOTFALL activation with genuine
audience participation. The activation should be the visual hero; do not lead with furniture or isolated equipment.
7. HOMEPAGE - IMMEDIATE SERVICE STRIP
Directly below the hero, show six clean categories using refined gold line icons:

DIGITAL EXPERIENCES
EVENT TECHNOLOGY
CROWD MANAGEMENT
EVENT ENVIRONMENTS
CONSULTATION & CONCEPT DEVELOPMENT

9. CASE STUDY STRUCTURE
Every major activation should become a structured FOOTFALL case study. Use the same format each time:
Event - where and when the activation happened.
Client / Partner - who the experience supported.
Challenge - what the client needed to achieve.
FOOTFALL Concept - the creative solution.
Activation - what was physically or digitally deployed.
Visitor Experience - how people interacted with it.
Measured Results - registrations, interactions or other verified metrics.
Gallery / Video - visual evidence.
Outcome - what the activation achieved for the brand.
10. EXPERIENCE / PROCESS SECTION
Headline: WE ENGINEER ATTENTION.
Display the five-stage FOOTFALL process horizontally on desktop and vertically on mobile:
01 ATTRACT - Create something impossible to ignore.
02 ENGAGE - Give people a reason to participate.
03 EXPERIENCE - Make the interaction memorable.
04 CAPTURE - Turn participation into measurable engagement.
05 CONNECT - Build a relationship beyond the event.
11. EXPERIENCES - PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE
Products should live inside experience categories, not as an unstructured rental list.
Experiential Activations: Giant Claw Machine, gift activations, custom interactive concepts, custom mascots and
custom plush toys.
Digital Experiences: Walking LED billboard bags, indoor LED walls, 43-inch touchscreen displays, custom LED
content and digital brand experiences.
Event Technology: PTZ cameras, standard controllers, video controllers, tablets, custom crowd-control software, QR
registration and visitor-registration systems.
Crowd & Visitor Management: Queueing systems, barricades, wristbands, visitor-flow systems and registration
management.
Event Environments: Exhibition lighting with dimmers, cocktail tables, high leather chairs, single-seater chairs, Elite
Tables and supporting event infrastructure.
Consultation & Concept Development: Activation strategy, creative consultation, visitor-engagement planning,
concept development, brand-experience strategy and custom event solutions.

20. UX / DEVELOPMENT DIRECTION
The site must be responsive across desktop, tablet and mobile. Prioritise fast loading, compressed media, clean
transitions and clear CTA placement.
Use subtle motion rather than excessive animation. Recommended effects include controlled image reveals, statistic
counters, short hover transitions, scroll-triggered process steps and restrained gold-line movement.
Video should be muted by default and optimised for web delivery. Provide static poster images as fallbacks. Text must
remain readable over all media using controlled overlays where necessary.
The website should feel premium and cinematic without sacrificing speed or usability.
21. CONTENT RULES
1. Do not describe FOOTFALL primarily as a rental company.
2. Lead with experience, attraction, participation and measurable engagement.
3. Use verified numbers only.
